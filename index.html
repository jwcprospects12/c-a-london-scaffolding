01 — Who we are

One of London's fastest growing scaffolding contractors.

Founded in 2021, C&A London Scaffolding started on residential work in north London. Consistent quality built an ever growing network of loyal customers, and that took us into commercial and industrial contracts across the capital.

Every scaffold we put up is erected by fully qualified and certified scaffolders, inspected before handover, and re-inspected every seven days while it stands. Safety is the number one priority and it underpins everything we do.

02 — Services

Every scaffold type we supply across London.

All services →
S/01

Commercial Scaffolding

Office blocks, retail units, hotels and mixed-use sites. Phased programmes that keep the building trading while work goes on.
S/02

Residential Scaffolding

Extensions, re-roofs, rendering and repointing on terraces, semis and flats. Neighbour-friendly, licence applications included.
S/03

Industrial Scaffolding

Factories, warehouses, plant rooms and utilities. Heavy-duty loadings, RAMS and drawings supplied before we mobilise.
S/04

Scaffold Tower Hire

Mobile and static towers delivered, erected and collected. Ideal for short access jobs, surveys and internal works.
S/05

Temporary Roofing

Keder and sheeted temporary roofs that keep a stripped building watertight through a London winter.
S/06

Access & Edge Protection

Staircases, walkways, crash decks and roof edge protection to keep other trades working safely at height.
S/07

Chimney Scaffolding

Chimney stack repairs, repointing and removal. Tight roof-level access with proper crash protection below.
S/08

Loft Conversion Scaffolding

Rear dormer and mansard access with loading bays sized for steels, joists and roof materials.
S/09

Birdcage & Internal Scaffolding

Full-floor working platforms for high ceilings, churches, atria and heritage interiors.
S/10

Loading Bays & Hoists

Designed loading platforms and hoist runs so materials reach the right lift without blocking the pavement.
S/11

Special Projects

Bridges, events, film sets and one-off geometry. If it can be drawn and calculated, we can build it.
